# Simplified Video Player

## Overview
A clean, minimalist video player website with only the essential features - your videos and the Inter font as requested.

## What's Included
- **Clean Design**: Black background with minimal interface
- **Inter Font**: Professional typography using Google Fonts
- **Your Videos**: Both provided videos are integrated
- **Essential Controls**: Play/pause, seek, volume, fullscreen
- **Video List**: Simple grid showing both videos with thumbnails
- **Responsive**: Works on desktop, tablet, and mobile

## How to Use
1. The first video loads automatically
2. Click on video thumbnails below to switch videos
3. Use standard video controls or keyboard shortcuts
4. Videos will auto-play the next one when finished

## Keyboard Shortcuts
- **Space/K**: Play/Pause
- **Left/Right Arrows**: Seek backward/forward 10 seconds
- **Up/Down Arrows**: Volume up/down
- **M**: Mute/Unmute
- **F**: Fullscreen
- **N/P**: Next/Previous video

The site now focuses purely on video playback with your content and the Inter font, removing all the extra elements you didn't need.
